🔰 Travel Safety Guidelines

  • 🎫 Always carry a valid ticket and ID proof
  • 👀 Keep your belongings secure and within sight
  • 📱 Keep your phone charged and emergency contacts handy
  • 🚪 Sit near exits or in well-lit areas during night travel
  • 👥 Avoid sharing personal information with strangers
  • ⏰ Arrive at stations/stops 15-20 minutes early
  • 🚰 Carry water and snacks for long journeys
  • 📋 Keep a copy of important documents
